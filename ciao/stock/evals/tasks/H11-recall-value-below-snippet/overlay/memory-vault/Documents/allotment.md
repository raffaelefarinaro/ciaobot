# Allotment

Plot 14 at the Birkenweg allotment garden. Yearly rent: CHF 180, paid each spring to the garden association.

## Plot

- Six by eight metres, south-facing, next to the shared water tap.
- The tool box padlock is the blue one; spare key with the neighbour on plot 15.
- Compost bins sit at the back fence and are emptied in October.

## Planting

- Beans and courgettes along the east edge, potatoes in the middle beds.
- Garlic goes in before the first frost; rotate the tomatoes every second year.
- Netting over the strawberries from May, or the blackbirds take them.

## Association

- Work days are the first Saturday of April and the last Saturday of September.
- Bonfires are not allowed; green waste goes to the municipal collection.
- Members vote on fees at the general assembly each September.

## Changes

- 2026 general assembly: from this season the fee is CHF 240, invoiced in February.
